## Getting Started

### Accessing the System
1. Open your web browser and navigate to the TrackGuests system URL provided by your administrator
2. You'll see the main dashboard with options to check in or view history

### First Time Setup
If this is your first time using the system, you may need to:
1. Register an account (if required by your organization)
2. Confirm your email address
3. Set up your profile preferences

## Check-In Process

### Step 1: Navigate to Check-In
1. From the main dashboard, look for the **"Check In"** button or link
2. Click on it to start the check-in process

### Step 2: Fill Out Your Information
Complete the check-in form with the following required information:

#### Personal Information
- **Name**: Your full name
- **Email**: Your email address
- **Phone**: Your contact phone number
- **Company**: Your organization or company name

#### Visit Details
- **Visitor Type**: Select from:
  - **Visitor**: External guests or clients
  - **Staff**: Employees or team members
  - **Resident**: Building residents or tenants

- **Purpose of Visit**: Brief description of why you're visiting
  - Examples: "Business meeting", "Delivery", "Maintenance", "Social visit"

#### Location Information
- **Room**: Select the room you're visiting from the dropdown menu
  - Rooms are organized by residence/building
  - Look for room numbers like "Room 101", "Conference Room A"
  - If you're unsure, check with reception or your host

#### Additional Information
- **FOB**: If you've been issued a key fob or access card, enter the number here
- **Memo**: Any additional notes or special instructions

### Step 3: Submit Your Check-In
1. Review all information for accuracy
2. Click the **"Check In"** button
3. You'll see a confirmation message with your check-in details
4. Note your check-in time for your records

### Check-In Tips
- ✅ **Be Accurate**: Double-check room numbers and contact information
- ✅ **Keep It Brief**: Purpose of visit should be concise but descriptive
- ✅ **Save Time**: The system may remember some of your information for future visits
- ✅ **FOB Numbers**: These are usually found on the physical access card/fob

## Check-Out Process

### Step 1: Locate Your Active Check-In
1. From the main dashboard, look for **"Check Out"** or **"Active Visits"**
2. You should see any active check-ins listed with your name and room

### Step 2: Select Check-Out
1. Find your current visit in the list
2. Click the **"Check Out"** button next to your entry
3. Confirm the check-out when prompted

### Step 3: Confirmation
1. You'll receive a confirmation that you've been checked out
2. The system will record your check-out time
3. Your visit is now complete and will appear in your history

### Check-Out Tips
- ✅ **Don't Forget**: Always remember to check out when leaving
- ✅ **Multiple Visits**: If you have multiple active check-ins, make sure to select the correct one
- ✅ **Return Fobs**: Remember to return any access cards or fobs to reception

## Viewing Your History

### Accessing Your Visit History
1. Click on **"History"** from the main navigation
2. You'll see a list of all your previous visits

### Understanding the History View
Your history shows:
- **Date & Time**: When you checked in and out
- **Duration**: How long your visit lasted
- **Room**: Which room/location you visited
- **Purpose**: The reason for your visit
- **Status**: Whether the visit is completed or still active

### Filtering Your History
- **Date Range**: Use the date picker to view visits from specific time periods
- **Default View**: The system typically shows the last 30 days
- **Export**: You may be able to export your history as needed

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### "Room Not Found" Error
- **Solution**: Check with reception for the correct room number
- **Tip**: Rooms may be listed by building or floor

#### "Already Checked In" Message
- **Cause**: You may already have an active check-in
- **Solution**: Check out of your previous visit first, or contact support

#### Email Confirmation Issues
- **Check Spam**: Confirmation emails may go to spam/junk folders
- **Verify Email**: Ensure you entered the correct email address
- **Contact Admin**: If problems persist, contact your system administrator

#### Access Card/FOB Problems
- **Wrong Number**: Double-check the number on your physical card
- **No Card**: If you don't have a card, leave the FOB field empty
- **Lost Card**: Report lost cards to reception immediately

#### Can't Find Your Room
- **Building Maps**: Check for building directories or maps
- **Ask Reception**: Front desk staff can help locate rooms
- **Contact Host**: Reach out to the person you're visiting

### Getting Help

#### Contact Information
- **Reception**: Contact your building's reception desk
- **IT Support**: For technical issues with the system
- **Administrator**: For account or access problems

#### Emergency Procedures
- **Emergency Exit**: In case of emergency, follow building evacuation procedures
- **Don't Wait**: Don't worry about checking out during emergencies
- **System Recovery**: The system can be updated manually if needed

### Best Practices

#### For Visitors
- ✅ Arrive a few minutes early to complete check-in
- ✅ Have your host's contact information ready
- ✅ Know the room number or meeting location in advance
- ✅ Bring identification if required by building security

#### For Staff/Regular Users
- ✅ Check in even for brief visits to maintain accurate records
- ✅ Update your contact information if it changes
- ✅ Report any system issues to IT promptly
- ✅ Help train new users on the process

#### For Everyone
- ✅ Be patient with new users learning the system
- ✅ Keep personal information up to date
- ✅ Follow your organization's visitor policies
- ✅ Report suspicious activity or security concerns
